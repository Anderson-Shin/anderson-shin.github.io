Visualizing the Change of Variables

An interactive journey to see how probability density transforms.

The Original Distribution, f(x, y)

We start with two independent variables, X and Y, both uniformly random between 0 and 1. Think of this as picking a random point inside a 1x1 square on the floor.

Base Area: 1 × 1 = 1

Probability Density (Height): 1

Total Probability (Volume): 1 × 1 = 1

The Transformed Distribution, f(u, v)

We create new variables: U = X+Y (the sum) and V = X-Y (the difference). This transformation rotates and stretches our original square.

New Base Area: 2

New Density (Height): 1/2 (due to Jacobian)

Total Probability (Volume): 2 × 1/2 = 1

Click and drag to rotate. Use the mouse wheel to zoom.
